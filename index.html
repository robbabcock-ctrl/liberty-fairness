Our principles

Liberty: government stays out of your life and your wallet unless it clearly earns its place. Fairness: rules and benefits apply equally, and nobody gains a right at someone else's expense.

Is it worth it?

Every tax, bond, or program must deliver more good than it costs. We show the full price, interest included.

Is it fair to all?

Benefits should be open to anyone who might need them someday, not carved out for a few.

Record over rhetoric

What candidates did in office counts more than what they say before an election. We flag last-minute shifts.

Where we stand on the issues
Taxes & spending
Low taxes, limited government, and a strong economy. Core services first: public safety, roads, courts.
Life
Protect life at every stage, with an exception to save the mother's life. The later the pregnancy, the stronger the protection.
Immigration
Welcome legal immigrants at a pace communities can absorb. Enforce the law, secure the border, no amnesty.
Equal rights
Live and let live, but no new right that costs others: keep women's sports and private spaces sex-based, and no compelled speech.
Personal freedom
Marriage, marijuana, and gambling are personal choices, not government business.
Guns
The Second Amendment means what it says.
Public safety
Back the police and hold them accountable. End civil asset forfeiture abuse.
Education
School choice and local control, with parents in charge.
Property
Owners have the right to build. Loosen zoning and cut red tape.
Energy
Reliable, affordable power from oil, gas, and nuclear, without mandates or subsidies.
Health care
A limited safety net for necessary care, citizens first.
